# A new website, and starting to write
I made this website because I wanted to start writing and documenting things that I did. I usually work on different projects, and I used to leave it undocumented. Documenting what you've learned and writing it down makes your understanding deeper by clearing your head and organizing your thoughts. This website is a way for me to getting started with writing.
I want this website to be a part of me, my thoughts, my memories, a collection of my life-events, lessons, and teachings.

Additionally, I've tried to get myself out of social media for quite a while now, but one way or another I keep going back to it---just for the sake of "connecting with friends & families who are far from me". I think the social media that we subscribed to is a thing of the past. Now, it's just a platform where we doomscroll strangers' posts and videos, and more recently AI slops. I used to justify not removing it because it let me "connect with friends & families". Now moving ahead, this website is an effort to create an archive of my thoughts, work, and memories in a personally owned space.
